ZOOSI

Teaching animal names through playful design

Zoosi helps little ones discover animal names through playful, geometric artwork and hands-on games. What began as a single set of flashcards has grown into a whole menagerie of ways to play in more than one language.

How Zoosi began

Zoosi started as a gift. Designer and illustrator Tou Yia Xiong went looking for something educational and Hmong-inspired for his nieces and nephews. He couldn’t find anything that was both culturally meaningful and beautifully made. So he made it himself: a set of flashcards teaching animal names in Hmong, his first language. Those cards became the heart of Zoosi.

Zoosi, pronounced Zoo-See, got its name from a combination of two Hmong words: good, which is zoo, and play, which is ua si. In direct translation, Zoosi means good play — which is at the core of what Zoosi is.

What’s inside

  • Lots of ways to play. Memory, Spelling Bee, and Flashcards today — with more games on the way.
  • Many languages. Learn animal names in English, Hmong White, Hmong Green, and Spanish. More languages to come.
  • Made to be seen. A flat, bold vector art style built for smooth play on both phone and tablet.
  • Rooted in culture. Hmong language and heritage sit at the very heart of the app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Zoosi is a free set of animal-learning games made by Tou Yia Xiong in Brooklyn, New York. This page explains what happens to information when you or your child use zoosi.co.

The short version: Zoosi has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, and no cookies. Nothing is tracked for advertising, and we never ask children for information about themselves.

Zoosi doesn’t ask for personal information

There are no accounts, logins, or profiles. Zoosi never asks for a name, email address, age, birthday, photo, location, or anything else about the person playing. There is no chat, no comments, no uploads, and no way for a player to send information to us or to anyone else through the games.

What the web server records automatically

Like nearly every website, the server that hosts Zoosi keeps a standard access log of the requests it receives. Each entry records:

  • the IP address the request came from
  • browser type and version, and operating system
  • the page or file requested
  • the date and time
  • the referring page, if you arrived by following a link

These logs are created by our hosting provider, GoDaddy, and summarized by AWStats, a statistics tool built into the hosting account. They are retained by the hosting provider for a limited period and are not archived or exported anywhere else.

No third-party analytics service, advertising network, or tracking script runs on Zoosi.

Notice about persistent identifiers

An IP address is what children’s privacy law calls a persistent identifier. Zoosi collects it only through the standard server log described above, and only to support the internal operations of the site. Specifically:

  • Keeping the site running — diagnosing errors, broken links, and files that fail to load.
  • Security — identifying and blocking automated attacks, scraping, and abuse of the server.
  • Understanding use — counting how many people visit and which games get opened, so that decisions about what to build next are informed.

These identifiers are not used to build profiles of visitors, to contact anyone, to serve advertising, or for any form of behavioral targeting. They are not sold, rented, or shared with anyone else for their own purposes, and they are not combined with any other information about a visitor. Zoosi has no mechanism for doing any of these things, because no advertising or analytics code is installed on the site.

Nothing is saved on your device

Zoosi does not store anything on the device you play on. No cookies are set, and nothing is written to your browser’s storage. Each time a game opens it starts fresh, which also means progress is not carried over between sessions.

Sound

Zoosi never requests access to your microphone and does not record sound.

Animal names in all four languages are prerecorded audio files stored on Zoosi’s own server. Playing them involves no one but you and us.

Other services Zoosi touches

  • Google Fonts. Zoosi’s pages load two typefaces, Baloo 2 and Nunito, from Google’s font service. When a page opens, your browser requests those files directly from Google, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request. Google’s privacy policy governs what they do with it.
  • Gumroad. Donations are handled entirely on Gumroad’s own site. If you choose to donate, you leave Zoosi and Gumroad’s privacy policy applies. We never see or handle payment card details.
  • Outbound links. Zoosi links to Instagram and to Tou Yia Xiong’s portfolio. Once you follow a link away from zoosi.co, that site’s own policy applies.

No advertising

Zoosi carries no advertising of any kind. There are no ad networks, no retargeting pixels, and no social media tracking pixels anywhere on the site. Nothing about a visitor is sold or shared for marketing.

Children’s privacy

Zoosi is made for young children and the grown-ups who play alongside them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the games are built so that there is nothing for a child to submit.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information some other way — by emailing the contact address below, for instance — write to us and we will delete it.

Parents and guardians

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold that relates to your child, to request that it be deleted, or to ask that we stop collecting it. Because Zoosi keeps no accounts and no records tied to individuals, in most cases the honest answer will be that there is nothing to retrieve or delete. We will tell you so plainly.

Visitors outside the United States

Zoosi is operated from the United States, and the server that hosts it is located there. If you visit from anywhere else, your request reaches a U.S. server and appears in the access log described above. Those logs are held by our hosting provider, are overwritten on their own schedule, and are never archived, exported, or combined with anything else. Beyond that log, Zoosi holds no accounts and no records tied to any individual, so there is nothing further about your visit held anywhere.

If you have a question about any of this, write to us using the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

If Zoosi ever adds something that changes this picture — a new analytics tool, a feature that saves work, anything at all — this page will be updated before that change goes live, and the date at the top will change with it.
